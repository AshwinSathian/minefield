Hints and odds

Hint finds a move you can be sure of and shows the numbers that prove it. Odds shades each hidden cell by its exact chance of being a mine, worked out from every arrangement that fits the board. Both stop the game counting toward your best times.

Pinch to zoom on large boards. Your game is saved as you play. Source on GitHub
